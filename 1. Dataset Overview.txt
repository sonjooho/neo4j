1. Key Entities and Relationships
  - Key Entities
    - Customer: Core customer data
    - Account: Banking account number
    - Card: Card number
    - CardIssuer: Card authority(e.g. Visa)
    - Merchant: Retailer
  - Relationships
    - OWNS_ACCOUNT: Customer -> Account
    - HAS_CARD: Customer -> Card
    - ISSUED_BY: Card -> CardIssuer
    - PURCHASED_AT: Card -> Merchant
    - TRANSFERRED_TO: Account -> Account

2. Why Graph Database?
Traditional relational databases require resource-heavy JOIN operations to uncover multi-hop relationships (e.g., A transfers to B, B transfers to C, C transfers back to A).
Graph-based analysis is uniquely positioned for the banking customer, allowing teams to instantly traverse highly connected data to detect sophisticated financial crimes, assess risk exposure in real-time, and generate profound, holistic customer insights.

3. Potential analytical use-cases
  - Fraud Detection: Instantly trace circular money movement and complex laundering paths.
  - Customer 360: Segment clients based on holistic spending behaviors across instruments.
